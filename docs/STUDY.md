# Study maps and wording

Generated by `tools/build_maps.py` from `tools/study_spec.py`. Do not edit by hand.

Everything the app says on each map, in study order. The announcement plays when a map opens
and again with a double tap. A two-finger double tap plays the information items in order, and a
two-finger swipe down or up reads the next or the previous item on its own. After a short pause
each item is followed by its place among the items played, for example "2 of 6".
In Trial 2 the researcher can switch each item off; the announcement always plays.

## Points for the team to confirm

- The requirements list seven optional items, including Intersection features (medians, bike lanes, islands). The 10/28 descriptions give text for six, so the app has six switches; the median at Fore and Silver is described in items 1 and 5.
- In Trial 2 the count after each item covers only the items switched on, for example "1 of 5" with one item off.
- Pearl Street: the Fore Street leg toward Silver Street is split by the tip of the Boothby Square island, crossed on paving stones. The practice map shows it as a median; the description calls the intersection plus-shaped.
- Gorham's Corner: aerial photos from 2025 show five zebra crosswalks, including the two across Pleasant Street and York Street added in 2023. The maps draw all five. The 2023 changes also set the signals to flash red as a five-way stop; the descriptions say signal controlled.
- Gorham's Corner: city records name the first stretch of the south leg Fore Street; the maps call it York Street, as the descriptions do.
- Commercial Street and Union Street: the leg opposite Union Street is Union Wharf, and the map names it so.
- Route overview: intersection 1 is drawn with Silver Street on both sides, like the other four; the mockup showed no cross street there.
- The crosswalks described as angled are drawn at their measured angles: about 24 degrees at Union and Fore, 26 at Exchange and Moulton, and 28 across Center Street, where York Street meets it at a sharp angle.
- Route overview: the cross street stubs are 13.5 mm, half again as long as before, except the two Fore Street stubs between intersections 2 and 4, which stay 9 mm: those squares are 26.5 mm apart, so longer stubs would meet. The training overview's stubs are 15 mm; to fit them, its Orange Road leg is 22 mm instead of 30.
- Intersections 3 and 4 now sit as near the middle of the screen as their whole walk allows, about 5 and 9 mm off centre. Intersection 1 and reversed 5 are also slightly off centre, about 7 mm, and could be treated the same way.
- Street names start beyond each street's crosswalk. On intersection 2 and reversed 2 all of Fore Street on screen lies between its crossings, so the road itself is not named there; its crosswalks and sidewalks still say Fore Street.
- Route overviews: where the route turns, the intersection's cue ends with "Turn.", for example "Intersection 4: 4-way intersection of Union Street and Fore Street. Turn."
- Intersection maps: the pink dots at the corners, where a crosswalk begins, keep their one ding and now vibrate like the sidewalk, as does the end of a crosswalk between the road and the corner, so a corner has no dead spot. The dots can be left out if the team prefers.
- Intersection maps: the orange turn dots still ding every 0.4 s while touched; they can ding once, like the centre, if the team prefers.
